# Airports and terminals (snapshot 2026-11-23)

Used by `index.html` (`AIR` table) for [[vietnam-friends-agent]].

| City | Airport | International | Domestic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saigon | Tân Sơn Nhất (SGN) | T2 | VietJet: T1 (Hall A, since 13 Jan 2026). Vietnam Airlines (since 17 May 2025), Bamboo, Vietravel, Sun PhuQuoc Airways, Pacific: T3. VASCO: T1 or T3 (sources differ). |
| Hà Nội | Nội Bài (HAN) | T2 (expanded, opened 19 Dec 2025) | T1 |
| Phú Quốc | Phú Quốc (PQC) | one passenger terminal | same terminal; a new Terminal 2 is being built for APEC 2027 (opening date sources conflict: Dec 2026 phase 1 vs Jul 2027) |

Trip legs (fixed): arrive Saigon 17 Dec (international assumed) → Phú Quốc 19 Dec → Hà Nội 24 Dec → Saigon 30 Dec → leave Saigon 2 Jan (international assumed).
